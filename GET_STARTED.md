## **WEEK 1-2: Immediate Actions (Do This First!)**

### ✅ **Step 1: Finalize Your Product** (Priority: CRITICAL)
**What to do:**
- [ ] Test every page on your website - click every link, test every feature
- [ ] Fix any bugs or broken links
- [ ] Ensure the AI agent works smoothly in all modes
- [ ] Test on mobile devices (most parents/students use phones)
- [ ] Make sure all contact information is accurate

**Why:** You can't sell a broken product. First impressions matter.

---

### ✅ **Step 2: Create Your "Sales Pitch" Document** (Priority: CRITICAL)
**What to create:** A professional PDF proposal (I'll help you create this)

**Include:**
1. **Executive Summary** - What you built and why it's valuable
2. **Features & Benefits** - What the website + AI agent does
3. **ROI Analysis** - How much money/time it saves Argyle
4. **Pricing Proposal** - Your initial sale price + ongoing maintenance
5. **Your Story** - Why you built this (shows passion and commitment)
6. **Demo Screenshots** - Visual proof of what you created

**Why:** Schools need documentation. They can't buy based on "trust me, it's cool."

---

### ✅ **Step 3: Identify Your "Champion" at Argyle** (Priority: HIGH)
**Who to talk to:**
- **Principal** - Final decision maker
- **Technology Director/Coordinator** - Technical approval
- **Assistant Principal** - Often handles operations
- **Your favorite teacher** - Can introduce you to admin

**Action items:**
- [ ] Make a list of 3-5 people you could approach
- [ ] Ask a teacher you trust to introduce you
- [ ] Schedule a 15-minute meeting (don't ask for money yet - just show what you built)

**Why:** You need someone inside who believes in you and can advocate.

---

### ✅ **Step 4: Prepare Your Demo** (Priority: HIGH)
**What to prepare:**
- [ ] 5-minute live demo script (show the best features)
- [ ] Backup: Record a video demo if live demo isn't possible
- [ ] Practice explaining: "I built this to help Argyle students..."
- [ ] Have answers ready for: "How does it work?" "How much does it cost?" "What if it breaks?"

**Why:** Seeing is believing. A good demo = instant credibility.

---

## **WEEK 3-4: The Pitch**

### ✅ **Step 5: Schedule Your Meeting**
**How to approach:**
1. Email or ask in person: *"I've built something for Argyle that I'd love to show you. Could I have 15 minutes of your time?"*
2. Be professional but enthusiastic
3. Don't mention money in the first meeting - just show value

### ✅ **Step 6: Present Your Proposal**
**During the meeting:**
- Show your demo (5 minutes)
- Explain the value (5 minutes)
- Answer questions (5 minutes)
- **End with:** "I'd love to discuss how we can make this work for Argyle. When would be a good time to talk about next steps?"

**After the meeting:**
- Send a thank-you email
- Attach your proposal PDF
- Follow up in 1 week if no response
